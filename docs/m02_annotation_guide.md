# M02 draft review policy v1

**Unit:** one feedback message. **Task:** mark the messages that staff should look at, based on the text you can see now. A `review` label does not approve a refund or any other action for the customer.

## Labels

- `review`: an open problem, or a clear request for help. A question that asks how to do something (a how-to question, such as "Could you explain how to …?") counts as a request for help.
- `no_review`: the problem is clearly solved, or the customer gives an opinion and needs no help.
- `unclear`: too little context to decide. Staff would ask the customer for more details.

## How to label

- Keep the message ID and quote a short evidence phrase, copied exactly from the message.
- Use only the message and the columns in the file. Do not invent a history for the customer.
- A star rating alone does not decide the label.
- FB012 ("Still waiting.") is the example of too little context under v1: label it `unclear`.
- If you disagree with the policy, write down the disagreement and your suggested wording, with a new version number such as v1.1. Do not change the labels quietly.

## Which messages to label

- Label FB001–FB012. They are part of the development partition.
- FB020 has exactly the same text as FB005, so it stays in development with FB005. It is not one of the 12 labeling tasks. Do not copy FB005's label to FB020: the same words show that the two rows belong in one group, not that one customer wrote both.
- Do not label the protected messages.
- FB022 has no text. It is left out of the labeling task.

## Limits

The groups and the partition are made-up example data. They do not link real customers, and they are not a benchmark for measuring accuracy. This lab needs no agreement score, API, web scraping or labeling platform.
